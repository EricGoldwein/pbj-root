PBJ320 for AI agents

PBJ320 makes federal nursing-home staffing and ownership data available in structured form for research assistants and AI agents.

Connect

Public read-only MCP endpoint:

https://www.pbj320.com/mcp

Machine-readable site guide: /llms.txt

What you can ask

  • Find nursing homes in Ohio by name or CCN.
  • Summarize latest quarterly staffing for CCN 366395.
  • Show facilities associated with a CMS owner PAC.
  • Compare two or more facilities by explicit CCN list.
  • Retrieve CMS PBJ daily evidence for a specific date and metric.

Sources

PBJ320 responses identify the underlying CMS source, reporting period, and PBJ320 methodology when a value is derived.

Source: CMS Payroll-Based Journal
Period: shown per response (quarter or work date)
Analysis: PBJ320 (when derived)
Evidence: get_staffing_evidence tool for audit-ready daily provenance

Methodology

Full definitions and dataset notes: Data sources & methodology.

Limitations

  • Read-only research API; not a bulk data export service.
  • Staffing quarters and ownership releases follow PBJ320 production release policy.
  • Daily evidence covers bundled PBJ quarters; Employee Detail is not included in MCP v0.
  • No geo-radius search or invented peer cohorts—use explicit CCNs or documented state percentiles.
